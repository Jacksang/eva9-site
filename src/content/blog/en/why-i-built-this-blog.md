After 20 years building software for other people — e-commerce platforms, payment gateways, mobile marketplaces — I realized something was missing.

A space that was truly my own.

## The Trigger

In early 2026, I was between consulting projects and reflecting on what I'd built over two decades. Millions of users. Billions in transaction volume. Teams across three countries.

But if you Googled my name, there was nothing coherent. A LinkedIn profile. Some old GitHub repos. Conference slides from 2019.

That felt wrong.

## What I Want This to Be

blog.eva9.ai is my attempt to create a digital home that reflects who I actually am:

- **A builder** — I make things. Code, systems, teams, businesses.
- **A learner** — At 40+, I'm studying ML and data science. You're never too old.
- **A thinker** — Not every insight is technical. Some are about people, decisions, trade-offs.
- **A bridge** — I think in both English and Chinese. My content should too.

## The Name

"eva9" comes from a personal mythology I've been building. In the movie Avatar, Eva is the protective deity of Pandora. The "9" represents multiplicity — 九, many forms, infinite potential.

eva9.ai is my domain for AI-powered tools and agents. This blog is the human side of that ecosystem.

## What to Expect

I'll be writing in the open about:

- **Work**: Cloud architecture, consulting lessons, technical decisions behind real systems
- **Learn**: My ML/data science journey, study notes, course reviews
- **Hobby**: Side projects, tools I build, experiments
- **Life**: Reflections on two decades in tech, career decisions, balance

Every post will have both English and Chinese versions. Not machine translated — actually written in both languages, with cultural nuance.

## The Tech

This site itself is a small engineering project:

- **Astro 5** for static site generation
- **Tailwind CSS 4** for styling
- **Lambda + DynamoDB** for dynamic features (comments, contact form)
- **S3 + CloudFront** for hosting
- **$0/month** operating cost (all AWS free tier)

I'll write a detailed post about the architecture soon.

## Let's See Where This Goes

I'm not chasing SEO or building an audience. I'm building a home for my ideas. If those ideas resonate with you — great. If not, that's fine too.

Welcome to blog.eva9.ai.
